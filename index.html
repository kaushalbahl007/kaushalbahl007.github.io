The developer
behind the bench.

Kaushal Kumar designs, codes, ships, and supports every title under Benzene Studio alone — from the first sketch of a mechanic to the last reply in an app-store review.

The name comes from the molecule: a small, stable ring that holds together something larger. Each app is built the same way — one clear mechanic, kept honest, without unnecessary friction.

One policy governs every title. Where an app differs, it's noted below.

Data Collected
No account creation is required, and no personally identifiable information is collected directly. Progress and content stay in local device storage unless an app explicitly offers optional cloud backup.
Advertising
Some titles display ads via Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.
Third-Party SDKs
Apps may use Google Play Services, subject to Google's Terms of Service. No other data-collection SDKs are used unless noted per app.
Children
All titles target general audiences and are not directed at children under 13; no personal data is knowingly collected from children.
Contact
For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, write to kaushalbahl007@gmail.com.
